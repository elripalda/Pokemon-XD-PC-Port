Rules
- Keep every % conversion (%d %s %u %.1f ...) and "\n" exactly, in a sensible order for the language.
- Don't translate button / key names that are written on the keyboard (F8, Tab, Alt+Enter, Esc, Enter), file names,
  setting ids in `backticks`, "GameCube", "Dolphin", "Wii", "Vulkan", "Direct3D", the names of programs.
- Tone: friendly and plain, as the English. Address the player formally-neutral: French "vous", German "Sie",
  Italian "tu" is used by Nintendo (use "tu"), Spanish "tú" is used by Nintendo (use "tú").
- The in-game pages are narrow: keep a translation about as long as the English where you can.
